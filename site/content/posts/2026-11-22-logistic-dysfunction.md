While the pure AI-empowered digital world puts out daily announcements of yet another unthinkable accomplishment, I feel that the same companies’ logistics are slumping. Consider two tech titans with mature, historically excellent operations.

### Amazon Prime shipping

It was and is a marvel. Receiving an item a few hours after you order it—as a standard service level!!—is amazing. But I feel they’ve been slipping. And they’re failing in weird ways. For example, everything is perfectly on schedule, until the last mile and suddenly it’s delayed for a few days. And in the more extreme cases, they simply can’t deliver the item to you... ever? The order is just stuck in some in-process state. Yet you can, mind-bogglingly, order the exact same item and get it that day. They’ll even recommend that you do just that! So… why not just ship me that second item? Feels broken.

### Apple support

My watch got smashed, but that’s covered under Apple Care so I wanted to send it in. The process to get that started? Absolute nightmare. Just endless loops linking “Let’s Get Started!” flows that lead know where, or drop me in some AI-assisted random knowledge base. When I finally got where I needed to be, it was insisting that the repair cost was $199 (not $69). At that point I called Apple, eventually punched my way through the AI clerk, and had a lady tell me it is $69 and that the page is wrong. 🤷‍♂️ … ok… 🤨

I sent the watch in and they received it over 2 weeks ago. Immediate it was classified as “Replace” (correct) and status is “Shipping soon”. Then… nothing. It just sits, no updates, nothing. I’m leaving on a trip soon and didn’t expect it to take a *month* to get a watch back. ### Time to Reassess

There an oft-mentioned design mantra:

> Make the easy things easy and the hard things possible

I feel like we’re getting to the point of making the hard thing easy and the easy things impossible.
